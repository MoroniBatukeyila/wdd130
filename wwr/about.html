Rapids

At Rapids, our mission is simple: to give you the ultimate white-water adventure. As a family-owned and operated business for over three decades, we combine deep passion with top-tier safety. Our veteran guides are dedicated to crafting unforgettable memories on the river every single trip

History

What started in 1995 as a single raft, a pickup truck, and an endless love for wild rivers has grown into a premier adventure outfit. For over 30 years, Rapids has remained family-owned and locally operated. Through changing seasons and thousands of miles of river, our core passion hasn't shifted: sharing the rush of white water with adventurers of all skill levels. From humble beginnings to leading thousands of guests down the river, our commitment to safety, camaraderie, and unforgettable journeys remains the heart of everything we do.
