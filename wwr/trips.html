Los Dientes del Diablo

Difficulty: Advanced

Duration: Approximately 4 hours

Are you looking for an exciting river challenge? Los Dientes del Diablo offers an adventurous rafting experience for visitors seeking energetic rapids and an unforgettable day outdoors.

Participants should meet the trip operator's experience, age, and safety requirements. Conditions on the river determine whether the adventure can operate.

Big Mallard

Difficulty: Intermediate

Duration: Approximately 3 hours

Big Mallard is an exciting option for adventurers who want to enjoy moving water while developing their paddling and teamwork skills.

Before the trip begins, participants receive safety instructions and guidance. Work together with your team and enjoy the natural beauty surrounding the river.

Half Day Adventure

Difficulty: Beginner to Intermediate

Duration: Approximately 4 hours

Want to experience rafting without committing to a full day? The Half Day Adventure offers an opportunity to enjoy the outdoors, learn about rafting, and share a memorable experience with friends or family.

This trip is suitable for newcomers who meet the operator's participation requirements. Guides provide instructions before departure.
